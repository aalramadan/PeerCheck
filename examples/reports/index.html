PeerCheck demo

PeerCheck checked the samples of its repository, with CoCoDet and EditLens for the reviews. The reports sit in its examples folder.

  • Papers (CSV)Format, anonymity, and hidden text of five sample submissions.
  • Reviews (CSV)The labels of CoCoDet and EditLens, a planted citation, and citation requests in 14 reviews. Six of them are reviews of two ICLR 2017 papers, two by people, two written by models, and two polished by models.
  • Conflicts of interest (CSV)Shared email domains and affiliations between authors and a program committee.
